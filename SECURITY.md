# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue** for security problems. Use GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/funsoi-hub/secure-vault/security/advisories/new) instead.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you think it has.
I aim to reply within 7 days and to fix confirmed high-severity issues within 30.

## Scope

In scope: this repository's code, its Docker image, and the live demo.
Out of scope: denial-of-service through traffic volume, findings that need a
compromised device or browser, and missing best-practice headers with no real impact.

Please only test against your own account on the demo, and don't access other
people's data. Good-faith research within these rules won't be pursued.
